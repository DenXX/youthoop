Team Standings

Washington State Youth Basketball · by grade and gender

How teams have fared against each other across 681 scored tournament games. Teams play different events against different opponents, so the order is set by an ELO score, which weighs each result by the strength of the opponent rather than just counting wins. Each club's teams are tracked separately by grade and subteam.

This is a scoreboard, not a verdict on a program — it says nothing about coaching, development, or whether a club is the right fit for your kid. More on why we think that matters.

Only games between two clubs in our directory count toward ELO, so a team's W–L record below may include more games than its ELO does. Teams with fewer than 5 ELO games are marked provisional.
